# Draft store listing

**Name:** Folio — Comfortable reading

**Short description:** Turn the web into a calmer place to read. Six paper-inspired palettes, private Reader Mode, and thoughtful typography.

**Description:**

Make a little room for reading. Folio softens harsh backgrounds, keeps text readable, and gives articles their own quiet reading room.

Choose Natural Paper, Classic White, Sepia, E-Ink, Dark Reading, or OLED Black. Adjust warmth, luminance, contrast, accent saturation, and optional image dimming. Photographs keep their colors; no whole-page inversion is used.

Open Reader Mode for supported articles: clear typography, source attribution, reading progress, adjustable width, and fullscreen. Return to the original page without a reload. Customize individual websites, follow your system appearance or a daily schedule, and keep a local backup of your settings.

Folio processes content locally. No accounts, analytics, subscriptions, or content uploads. Automatic website access is optional and controlled through Chrome’s permission prompt. Browser-protected pages and some embedded or complex applications cannot be transformed. E-Ink is a visual palette, not reflective display hardware.

**Permission rationale:** activeTab for requested page access; scripting for local page tools and dynamically registered opted-in sites; storage for preferences; alarms for day/night switching. Optional host access enables automatic operation after navigation. All-site access is optional and requires a separate explicit request.

**Included assets:** `public/icons/128.png` and `dist/icons/128.png` are store-size icons. `screenshots/` contains actual UI captures of local fictional content. Supply publisher/support/privacy-policy URLs before submission. Promotional marketing imagery is optional and has not been fabricated as an actual store listing.
